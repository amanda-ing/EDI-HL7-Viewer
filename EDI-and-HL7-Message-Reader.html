How to read these messages (quick primer)

Segment – one line (or one record) of the message. It starts with a 2–3 letter code, like PID (patient) or PO1 (purchase order line).

Field / Element – one piece of data inside a segment. Its position is written like PID-5 (HL7: 5th field of PID) or PO1-03 (X12: 3rd element of PO1).

Component / Sub-component – some fields hold several parts, e.g. a name is LAST^FIRST^MIDDLE. Parts are written PID-5.1, PID-5.2…

  • HL7 usually uses | between fields, ^ between parts, ~ between repeats, & between sub-parts and \ for escape codes. The real characters are set in the MSH segment.
  • X12 usually uses * between elements, ~ at the end of each segment and : or > between parts. The real characters are set in the ISA segment.
  • Two separators side by side (|| or **) means that field was left empty.
  • In HL7, a value of "" (two quote marks) is an explicit null. It tells the receiving system to delete whatever it has stored for that field.
  • Segments starting with Z (like ZRX) are custom segments defined by your facility or vendor. Ask your interface team for their meaning.

Field names follow HL7 v2.5.1 and X12 004010/005010. Your trading partner's implementation guide can add to or change these meanings.
